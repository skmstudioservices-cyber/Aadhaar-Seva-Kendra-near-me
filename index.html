Aadhaar Seva Kendra near you — the official, scam-free way

Aadhaar appointments are FREE on the official UIDAI portal. Here's exactly how to book, what to carry, and how to dodge agents who charge extra.

⚡ Quick tips

  • Appointments on the official UIDAI site are FREE. Anyone charging a "booking fee" is overcharging you.
  • Book only on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in — search "Aadhaar Seva Kendra near me" on Google Maps to find the centre, but do the booking yourself online.
  • Mobile number updates can ONLY be done at a centre — never via email or phone calls.
  • UIDAI helpline: 1947. Official site: uidai.gov.in.

🖱 How to book an Aadhaar appointment (official, free)

  1. Go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and check the "Book an Appointment" option.
  2. Enter your Aadhaar number (or enrolment ID) and the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
  3. Pick your city — Aadhaar Seva Kendras run in many cities, and local enrolment centres (banks/post offices) are listed too.
  4. Choose the service, date and time slot, and take a printout or screenshot of the appointment slip.

No account? No OTP possible? — for fresh enrolment (first-time Aadhaar), locate the nearest enrolment centre via UIDAI's "Locate an Enrolment Center" page and walk in; many centres accept walk-ins.

🧾 Services at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra

  • New enrolment (first Aadhaar — free)
  • Demographic updates — name, address, date of birth, gender, email (first-time address update online is free on the portal; changes at the centre carry a nominal official fee)
  • Biometric updates — fingerprints, iris, photo. Adults who enrolled as children must update biometrics; UIDAI now also mandates a biometric update after age 5 and once after 18, and periodically thereafter.
  • Mobile number update / registration — centre-only, ₹50 official fee. No email or phone process exists — anyone claiming otherwise is a scam.
  • Aadhaar-PAN linking, mAadhaar app help, e-Aadhaar downloads.

Fees: mandatory biometric updates carry a ₹50 official fee. Enrolment is free. Pay only at the centre counter — never to an individual.

📄 Documents to carry

>

For updates, carry your Aadhaar + one supporting document for what's changing:

  • Proof of identity: passport, voter ID, driving licence, PAN, NREGS card etc.
  • Proof of address: passport, bank passbook, utility bill (recent), rental agreement etc.
  • Proof of date of birth: birth certificate, passport, Class 10 certificate, PAN.

Full valid-document lists are on uidai.gov.in — check before your visit to avoid a second trip.

📱 mAadhaar & online self-service

>

Many tasks never need a centre: download e-Aadhaar, order a PVC Aadhaar (₹50), update address online (with a valid address proof or via address-validation letter), lock/unlock biometrics, and check enrolment/update status. The mAadhaar app keeps your Aadhaar on your phone.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I book an Aadhaar Seva Kendra appointment near me?

Go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in, choose Book an Appointment, verify with OTP, and select your city, service and slot. Booking is free.

Is Aadhaar update free?

First enrolment is free, and your first address update online is free. Biometric and mobile updates at centres carry a ₹50 official fee. Agents charging extra are overcharging.

How do I find an Aadhaar centre near me?

Use UIDAI's 'Locate an Enrolment Center' page or search 'Aadhaar Seva Kendra near me' on Google Maps — then book the appointment yourself on the official portal.

Can my Aadhaar mobile number be updated online?

No. Mobile updates can only be done at an enrolment centre for a ₹50 fee. UIDAI never asks for your mobile OTP over phone or email.

What is the Aadhaar helpline number?

1947 is UIDAI's official helpline for Aadhaar queries and complaints.

⚠️ Scam alert: UIDAI never asks for your OTP, PIN or passwords by phone, email or SMS. Never share Aadhaar OTPs with anyone. Beware of agents offering "faster" services for extra money — the official portal (myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in) is the only place to book, and it's free.
